# MineMentor Dataset Provenance & Attribution

This document outlines the origin, licensing, and processing lineage of all datasets integrated into the **MineMentor** artificial intelligence system, fulfilling strict open-source provenance standards.

---

## 1. Primary Instruction Dataset

- **Dataset Name**: `Minecraft_QA-pairs_Instruction_Dataset`
- **Repository**: [https://huggingface.co/datasets/Aiwensile2/Minecraft_QA-pairs_Instruction_Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Aiwensile2/Minecraft_QA-pairs_Instruction_Dataset)
- **Authors**: Aiwensile2
- **DOI**: [10.5281/zenodo.11568799](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.11568799)
- **License**: [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/)
- **Original Sources**: Scraped from the Minecraft Wiki (`minecraft.wiki`) and Minecraft Fandom Wiki (`minecraft.fandom.com`).
- **Original Size**: 390,317 instruction-answer pairs (126.3 MB uncompressed JSON).
- **Generation Method**: Synthetically generated by prompting OpenAI's `gpt-3.5-turbo` over raw scraped wiki text and tables.
- **Cleaning & Modifications Applied by MineMentor**:
  1. Purged 200 non-string/float `NaN` corrupted records.
  2. Filtered out boilerplate one-word answers (`True`, `False`, `4`, hex color codes such as `#3F76E4`).
  3. Filtered out historical changelog and snapshot trivia (`snapshot`, `pre-release`, `datafixerupper`, `18w19a`, `1.13-pre5`) which heavily skewed the dataset with irrelevant mechanics.
  4. Normalized Unicode text artifacts (e.g. `22` grid dimensions repaired to `2x2`, curly apostrophes, smart quotes).
  5. Stripped prompt-leakage context clauses (e.g. *"According to the provided text..."*).
  6. Deduplicated identical queries, retaining the most informative answer.
  7. Formatted into stratified 80/10/10 Train, Validation, and Test splits with multi-class intent and difficulty labeling.

## 2. Official Crafting Recipes Dataset

- **Dataset Name**: `MinecraftRecipes`
- **Repository**: [https://huggingface.co/datasets/ProfQu/MinecraftRecipes](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ProfQu/MinecraftRecipes)
- **Author**: ProfessorQu
- **Source Repository**: [https://github.com/ProfessorQu/MCCraftingRecipes](https://github.com/ProfessorQu/MCCraftingRecipes)
- **License**: **MIT License**
- **Original Source**: Decompiled directly from official vanilla Minecraft Java source code.
- **Size**: 836 complete crafting table recipes across 16 recipe types.
- **Role in MineMentor**: Serves as the authoritative ground-truth catalog for the **Deterministic Calculation Engine** (crafting grid verification, resource decomposition, and material aggregation).

## 3. Minecraft Knowledge Corpus (Wiki Text)

- **Dataset Name**: `Minecraft-Wiki-2023`
- **Repository**: [https://huggingface.co/datasets/KoalaBrainResearcher/Minecraft-Wiki-2023](https://huggingface.co/datasets/KoalaBrainResearcher/Minecraft-Wiki-2023)
- **Authors**: KoalaBrainResearcher
- **License**: CC BY-SA 3.0 / CC BY-NC-SA
- **Original Source**: Full raw dump of the community-maintained Minecraft Wiki (`minecraft.wiki`).
- **Size**: 43.1 MB (`articles.jsonl`).
- **Role in MineMentor**: Chunked knowledge corpus for dense vector indexing and BM25 sparse keyword search in the hybrid RAG architecture.

## 4. Citation & Reference Index

- **Dataset Name**: `minecraft-question-answer-700k`
- **Repository**: [https://huggingface.co/datasets/naklecha/minecraft-question-answer-700k](https://huggingface.co/datasets/naklecha/minecraft-question-answer-700k)
- **Author**: Mayank Naklecha (`naklecha`)
- **License**: CC BY-NC-SA 3.0
- **Size**: 694,814 Q&A pairs.
- **Role in MineMentor**: Cross-referenced for exact Wikipedia URL citations (`source: https://minecraft.wiki/w/...`).

## 5. Curated Evaluation Benchmark

- **Dataset Name**: `MineMentor Expert Benchmark`
- **File**: `data/evaluation/expert_benchmark.json`
- **Authors**: MineMentor Core Research
- **License**: MIT
- **Design**: Completely held-out, expert-reviewed benchmark covering:
  - Beginner survival fundamentals
  - Exact resource calculations
  - Edition-specific divergence (Java vs. Bedrock)
  - Common player troubleshooting (mob spawning, villager mechanics)
  - Progression gating (Nether preparation, diamond prioritization)
  - Community trick questions and mod confusion (e.g. Aether portal glowstone trap, chainmail crafting)
